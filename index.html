⚖️ VidVids: Intellectual Property Policy

1. Content Sources
VidVids is a video discovery platform. All video content is embedded from YouTube using their official embed API and complies with YouTube’s Terms of Service. We do not host or store any video files on our servers.

2. No Background Music
VidVids does not play any background music. All audio comes exclusively from the YouTube videos that users choose to watch.

3. Respect for Copyright
We respect the intellectual property rights of others. If you believe a YouTube video embedded in VidVids infringes your copyright, you should follow YouTube’s own copyright complaint process. Alternatively, you may contact us, and we will remove the embedded link upon proper notice.

4. DMCA & Takedown Requests
If you are a copyright owner and believe that an embedded YouTube video violates your rights, please submit your claim via Telegram to support with the subject line: "Copyright Takedown Request."
Please include:
• The specific YouTube URL that appears in VidVids.
• A description of the copyrighted work.
• Your contact information.
• A statement of good‑faith belief that the use is not authorised.

5. Disclaimer
VidVids does not claim ownership of any YouTube content. All trademarks, brands, and video titles belong to their respective owners. We act solely as a neutral index and do not control the content of embedded videos.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Data We Collect
When you use VidVids, we may collect the following information:

  • Telegram User Data: Your Telegram user ID, username, first name, last name (if provided), and profile photo (if accessible).
  • Premium Status: Whether you have purchased a premium subscription, along with the expiration date of your premium access.
  • Payment Records: Transaction IDs, amount paid, currency, and timestamp of successful payments (for premium purchases).
  • Ad Interaction: Number of ads watched per day and the timestamp of the last ad viewed (used to enforce cooldown and daily limits).
  • Usage Data: Categories you browse, search queries (if any), and video view history (stored locally on your device).

How We Use Your Data
Your information is used solely to:

  • Provide and maintain the core functionality of the mini app.
  • Manage premium subscriptions and deliver ad‑free experiences to paying users.
  • Serve relevant ads and limit ad frequency to respect your experience.
  • Improve content discovery and app performance.
  • Respond to support inquiries or copyright claims.

Data Sharing
We do not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may share limited data (e.g., user ID) with our payment provider (Telegram Stars) to complete transactions. Aggregated, anonymised statistics may be used for internal analytics.

Data Retention
Your Telegram user data is retained as long as you use the app. Premium records are kept for financial and legal compliance. You may request deletion of your data by contacting support.

Your Rights
You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, please contact us via Telegram support.

Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the app constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
